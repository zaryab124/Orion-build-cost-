Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our quantity surveying methodology, turnaround times, and file requirements.

What file formats can I submit for cost estimation?
We work with standard architectural and engineering formats including PDF drawings, AutoCAD DWG/DXF files, Revit/BIM 3D models, sketches, and planning portal application documents. Even if your drawings are preliminary, we can measure elemental quantities and provide indicative cost models.
What is your turnaround time for a full Bill of Quantities?
Standard turnaround is 48 to 72 hours for residential extensions, single dwellings, and small commercial fit-outs. For urgent contractor tender deadlines, our Express 24-Hour service ensures you never miss a bid submission. Complex commercial schemes (over £5M) typically take 5 to 7 working days.
Are your estimates accepted by UK banks and mortgage lenders?
Yes, 100%. Our reports are drafted in accordance with RICS New Rules of Measurement (NRM2) and Standard Method of Measurement (SMM7). They are regularly submitted to tier-1 high street banks (Barclays, Lloyds, NatWest) and specialist development lenders for initial fund approval and monthly drawdowns.
Can you help identify value engineering cost savings?
Absolutely. Every estimate includes our complimentary Value Engineering assessment. We highlight structural framing alternatives, foundation optimizations, exterior cladding substitutions, and procurement strategies that typically reduce overall construction expenditure by 8% to 15% without sacrificing architectural vision.
