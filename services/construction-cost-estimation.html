Service Description

Informed Decision-Making at Every Project Milestone

Uncertainty is the greatest financial risk in construction. At Orion Build Cost, our construction cost estimating services provide developers, contractors, and self-builders with complete financial clarity before breaking ground. By synthesizing spatial design, structural parameters, current supply chain price movements, and regional labor rates, our cost plans prevent budget blowouts and empower clients to negotiate tenders with supreme confidence.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

Order of Cost Estimates (NRM1)

Early-stage feasibility cost models benchmarked against functional floor areas and building types to establish initial viability before planning fees are committed.

Elemental Cost Plans

Detailed breakdown of expenditure across key building components: Substructure, Superstructure, Internal Finishes, Fittings, Mechanical & Electrical, and External Works.

Value Engineering & Options

Identification of cost-efficient structural alternatives, cladding substitutions, and procurement strategies to lower expenditure without compromising architectural quality.

Cash Flow & Drawdown Schedules

Anticipated monthly capital expenditure profiles aligned with construction programme milestones to support lender loan facilities and equity funding.

Risk & Contingency Quantification

Objective contingency profiling separating design reserve, site ground risk, statutory planning allowances, and inflation risk.

Executive Summary Report

Publication-grade PDF and Excel documentation featuring graphical charts, rate per m² benchmarks, and clear narrative assumptions for executive stakeholders.

Example Cost Estimation Workflow

1

Project Brief & Spatial Analysis

We assess client design objectives, planning constraints, site access conditions, and target finish tiers.

2

Issuance of the comprehensive cost plan document with interactive Excel spreadsheet and presentation walkthrough.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what stage should I commission a construction cost estimate?
The earlier, the better. An early Order of Cost estimate during concept design (RIBA Stage 2) prevents spending thousands on architectural drawings for a scheme that exceeds the project budget. As drawings advance to technical design (RIBA Stage 3/4), the estimate is updated to an Elemental Cost Plan.
How are regional location differences accounted for?
We apply established UK regional indexing based on construction industry market data. Construction costs in Central London and the South East reflect higher labour and logistics expenditure, whereas regions in the Midlands, North, Wales, and Scotland reflect localized regional wage and plant rates.
Can I test cost scenarios myself right now?
Yes! You can use our online Estimating Engine to input your project floor area, choose specification tiers, and edit individual trade lines live in your browser.
