Service Description

Transparent Budgeting for Residential Building Projects

Building or extending a home is one of the most substantial financial investments an individual or small builder can undertake. Too often, residential construction suffers from wide variations between contractor quotes, uncosted structural requirements, and unexpected builder variations mid-construction. At Orion Build Cost, our residential estimating service produces clear, itemised breakdowns that provide realistic cost boundaries, empower homeowners to sanity-check builder quotes, and enable builders to submit professional, transparent proposals.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

Trade-by-Trade Cost Schedule

Itemised cost breakdown covering Demolition, Groundworks, Brickwork, Roofing, Joinery, Plastering, Plumbing & Heating, Electricals, and Decoration.

Builder Quote Sanity Check

Objective third-party analysis comparing multiple builder quotations against independent Quantity Surveying benchmark rates to reveal omissions or inflated allowances.

Material vs. Labour Separation

Clear distinction between trade labour hours and merchant material purchase costs, allowing self-builders to manage their procurement efficiently.

Specification Tier Benchmarking

Alternative finishes modeling comparing Standard Contractor spec, Premium Developer spec, and High-End Architectural luxury finishes.

Stage Payment Forecast

Recommended stage payment schedule linked to physical build milestones (e.g. Ground slab complete, Wall-plate level, Weathertight, Plaster complete).

VAT Assessment Advice

Clarification of applicable VAT rates: 20% Standard on extensions, 5% Reduced for long-term empty properties, or 0% Zero-rated for qualifying new dwellings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do quotes from different builders vary so wildly?
Quotes vary because builders interpret drawings differently. One builder may include high-spec underfloor heating, structural steel pads, and decoration, while another excludes ground disposal, scaffolding, or VAT. Our residential estimate establishes an unarguable baseline so you know what is truly included.
What VAT rate applies to my residential building work?
Standard extensions, loft conversions, and repairs incur standard 20% UK VAT. However, new residential home builds qualify for 0% zero-rated VAT under HMRC Notice 708. Conversions of premises empty for over 2 years qualify for reduced 5% VAT. We help identify the correct statutory treatment.
