Service Description

Institutional Rigour for Commercial Real Estate Schemes

Commercial construction demands strict alignment between tenant lease obligations, developer target yields, and senior funder appraisal models. At Orion Build Cost, our commercial estimating practice delivers institutional-grade cost planning across the entire asset lifecycle. From early residual land evaluations and Section 106 / CIL analysis through to bank-grade Bills of Quantities and contractor tender negotiations, we protect your development margins at every stage.

1

Spatial & MEP Specification Appraisal

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the distinction between Cat A and Cat B fit-out pricing?
Cat A (Developer Shell to Lettable Standard) includes raised floors, basic suspended ceilings, perimeter air conditioning, primary lighting, and core toilets. Cat B (Tenant Occupancy) encompasses private meeting rooms, bespoke acoustic glazing, brand decoration, teapoint kitchens, audiovisual cabling, and specialized desking infrastructure. We can price either package independently or as a unified contract.
How do you account for UK Domestic Reverse Charge VAT in commercial tenders?
For B2B commercial contracting between VAT-registered and CIS-registered businesses, VAT is accounted for under the UK Construction Services Domestic Reverse Charge rules. Our estimating engine supports an explicit "Reverse Charge / Excluded" setting to ensure B2B subcontracts avoid incorrect VAT double-accounting.
Can our commercial fund request custom work package breakdowns?
Yes. We regularly tailor our cost schedules to align directly with client accounting codes, internal capital expenditure categories, and development loan draw milestones.
