Service Description

Winning Profitable Bids Without Compromising Margins

Tendering for commercial and residential construction projects in the UK requires speed, precision, and rigorous risk control. Too many builders fail to win bids because their prices are inflated by unmeasured risks, or worse, win contracts on under-priced figures that wipe out their profit margin. At Orion Build Cost, our tender pricing service acts as your external estimating department: analysing client addenda, market-testing supply chain packages, and crafting compliant bids that stand out to procurement panels.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

Tender Document Review

Comprehensive analysis of drawings, specifications, Employer's Requirements (ERs), site investigation reports, and contract conditions (JCT/NEC).

Subcontractor Package Enquiries

Breaking down complex schemes into neat trade packages (groundworks, M&E, drylining, structural steel) with clear scopes of works for supply chain pricing.

Subcontractor Bid Comparison

Side-by-side equalisation of subcontractor quotations, identifying hidden exclusions, qualifications, and pricing anomalies between competing quotes.

Site Preliminaries Build-Up

Accurate itemisation of site management staff, temporary fencing, welfare cabins, plant craneage, scaffolding durations, and skip logistics.

Margin & Overhead Modeling

Transparent application of contractor head-office overheads, net profit margins, and specific commercial risk reserves.

Complete Submission Pack

Filled-in Form of Tender, priced Bill of Quantities, schedule of rates for future variations, and formal qualification covering letters.

Example Tender Pricing Workflow

1

Tender Document Appraisal & Query Log

We register all drawings and create a Requests for Information (RFI) log to clarify architectural ambiguities early with the client's design team.

2

Direct Material & Subcontractor Pricing

We issue enquiries to preferred or local trade subcontractors, measure internal take-offs, and price self-delivered work packages.

3

Tender Adjudication & Margin Review

We present the prime cost breakdown to your commercial directors to agree final preliminaries, overheads, risk contingency, and target margin.

4

Final Bid Package Delivery

We populate the client's pricing schedules and draft the qualification letter ready for submission well before the procurement deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you turnaround a contractor tender price?
Turnaround depends on project scale and completeness of drawings. For single trade packages and small-to-medium tenders, we typically turnaround complete bid models within 3 to 5 working days. Express turnarounds can be coordinated when tender deadlines are imminent.
Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?
Yes, absolutely. Commercial confidentiality is paramount. We are happy to review and execute your standard bilateral NDA before receiving tender documents, ensuring your pricing margins, supply chain relationships, and bid strategies remain private.
Can you assist with post-tender negotiations?
Yes. If you are shortlisted to the final two contractors, we can help prepare value engineering alternatives and schedule adjustments to secure the award without eroding your core profit margins.
