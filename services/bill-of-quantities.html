Service Description

Transparent Cost Packaging for Competitive Tenders

A professionally drafted Bill of Quantities provides a comprehensive, itemised schedule of all work, labour, plant, and materials required to deliver your project. By establishing a single, unarguable baseline for all tendering contractors, a BOQ eliminates scope ambiguity, exposes hidden tender exclusions, and serves as the definitive financial schedule for valuing monthly interim valuations and project variations during construction.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

NRM2 Work Package Structure

Organised systematically into standard UK work sections: Groundworks, Concrete Works, Masonry, Structural Framing, Cladding, MEP Services, and Finishes.

Preliminaries & Site Overheads

Comprehensive schedule of main contractor preliminaries: project supervision, site accommodation, temporary services, scaffolding, waste logistics, and statutory health & safety.

Provisional Sums & Contingencies

Defined and undefined provisional sums for elements requiring future design development, alongside transparent contingency risk allowances.

Tender Comparison Matrix

Blank contractor unpriced tender return schedules alongside our benchmark priced model, allowing like-for-like bidding comparison between contractors.

Valuation Payment Schedules

Structured milestone drawdown schedules aligned with JCT or NEC building contract terms for bank monitoring surveyors and contract administrators.

Multi-Format Deliverables

Delivered in fully editable Excel (.xlsx) workbooks with formula links, structured CSVs, and branded print-ready A4 executive summary PDFs.

Example BOQ Workflow

1

Drawing & Specification Appraisal

Review of all architectural layouts, structural engineers' drawings, and employer's requirements to define the boundary of works.

2

NRM2 / SMM7 Work Section Measurement

Quantities are extracted according to standard UK measurement rules, with each item assigned a unique item number, standard unit, and exact description.

3

Rate Application & Cost Modeling

Illustrative benchmark rates or verified contractor market prices are applied to generate section subtotals, contingency, and VAT allowances.

4

Tender Pack Compilation

Issuance of the unpriced tender document for contractors, priced internal estimate for client cost planning, and comparison dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Quantity Take-Off and a Bill of Quantities?
A Quantity Take-Off extracts the raw dimensions and material quantities (e.g. m² of brickwork or m³ of concrete). A Bill of Quantities organizes those quantities into formalized contract packages with legal preliminaries, workmanship descriptions, provisional sums, and unit rate pricing for tender competition.
Can lenders and bank monitoring surveyors review this document?
Yes. Our Bills of Quantities are prepared strictly in accordance with RICS NRM2 principles, which is the exact standard required by UK high street banks, private debt funds, and institutional lenders for loan facility approvals and monthly drawdown sign-offs.
Can I test or edit the BOQ online before requesting a quote?
Yes! You can explore our live BOQ Builder tool to customize trade items, view category subtotals, and download a verified Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet sample immediately.
